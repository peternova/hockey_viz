# Content outline

Everything planned for the app. It's for checking scope: **cross out what you don't want and add what's missing.**
Labels use Australian/NZ wording. US/UK alternatives are shown in brackets.

> ⚠️ **Position names differ between clubs, even within Australia.** In a back four, many AU/NZ clubs call the wide defenders "left half / right half" and the central defenders "fullbacks". Those names are left over from the old 2-3-5 formation. The app shows both names. Tell me what *your* club says and I'll make that the main label.

## 1. Positions (3 × 3 grid + goalkeeper)

The default team shape is **4-3-3** (4 defenders, 3 midfielders, 3 forwards, plus the GK).

| | Left | Centre | Right |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Forward** | Left wing (left forward) | Centre forward (striker) | Right wing (right forward) |
| **Midfield** | Left midfield (left inner / left link) | Centre half (centre mid) | Right midfield (right inner / right link) |
| **Defence** | Left back (left half) | Centre back ×2 (fullback / sweeper) | Right back (right half) |

Every position page has the same sections:
1. **Where you live:** a zone on the pitch, animated to show how it shifts in attack, defence and when the ball switches sides.
2. **The four moments:**
   - We have the ball: support angles, width/depth, when to go forward.
   - They have the ball: who you mark, which way you channel, when you press.
   - We just won it: your first look and your first run.
   - We just lost it: delay, recover, get back into shape.
3. **Set pieces:**
   - Penalty corner attack (injector, stopper, shooter, post, top).
   - Penalty corner defence (GK, first runner, post, second runner, trail/cover).
   - Long corner, centre pass, defensive free hit (outlet), free hits inside the attacking 23 m.
4. **Left / right specifics.** Hockey is asymmetric because you only play with the flat (left) face of the stick.
   - On the **right**, your forehand (open stick) faces the side-line.
   - On the **left**, receiving and passing need more reverse stick, and the body position is different.
   - Defenders on each side channel attackers differently.
5. **Key skills** (3–5 per position).
6. **Common mistakes** (3–5 per position).
7. **"Play smart" tips for experienced players:**
   - Anticipate rather than chase.
   - Position early so you don't have to sprint.
   - Talk and organise the players in front of you.
   - Use rolling subs on purpose.
   - Know when *not* to press.
8. **Linked plays** (from section 2).

The **goalkeeper** gets a short page: how the keeper and the defenders work together, plus calls and organisation.

## 4. Pitch Explorer (tap a marking or area to learn it)

- **Lines:** side-line, back-line, goal-line, centre-line, 23 m lines.
- **Circle area:** the circle (shooting circle / "the D"), the dotted line 5 m outside it, the penalty spot.
- **Marks:** back-line marks at 5 m and 10 m (penalty corner injection), side-line marks at 14.63 m.
- **Areas:**
  - Thirds of the pitch, and the corridors (left / centre / right channels).
  - The 23 m area and its special rules: no direct entry into the circle from a free hit, the 5 m rule, the long corner restart, and the goalkeeper must stay inside it.

## 6. "Where should I be?" quiz (~12 situations to start)

The app freezes a moment and you drag your player to where you think they should be. For example:
- Opposition free hit on your 23 m line, right side, and you're the **left back**.
- Your centre back has the ball and you're the **right midfielder**.
- You've just lost the ball in midfield and you're the **centre forward**.
- Attacking penalty corner (P2) and you're on the **post**.

Plus quick quizzes matching terms to pictures, and rule true/false questions.

## 7. Coach tactics board

- Drag the 22 players and the ball, starting from a formation template.
- Record steps, add captions and arrows (run, pass, dribble).
- Play it back with the same animation engine.
- Plays are saved on the device and can be exported/imported as a file.
